# Tenacious Tone Style Guide

This guide defines the required tone markers for all outbound content generated by the Conversion Engine.
The NurtureSequencer scores every draft against these markers before dispatch (max 2 regeneration retries).

---

## Core Tone Markers

### 1. Professional but Direct
- Lead with the finding, not the preamble.
- No filler phrases: "I hope this email finds you well", "Just reaching out to…", "I wanted to touch base…"
- State the reason for contact in the first sentence.

### 2. Grounded in Data
- Every claim must reference a specific signal from the HiringSignalBrief.
- Do not assert trends without evidence (e.g., do not say "you're growing fast" without a job-post count).
- Use exact figures when available: "14 open engineering roles" not "many open roles".

### 3. No Over-Claiming
- Do not use superlatives: "best", "fastest", "most innovative".
- Do not assert AI maturity assertively when confidence is low — use interrogative phrasing: "we noticed signals suggesting…"
- Do not claim "aggressive hiring" unless job_post_count ≥ 5 AND job_post_velocity_60d ≥ 3.0.
- Do not reference competitor gaps unless CompetitorGapBrief gap confidence is medium or high.

### 4. No Jargon
- Avoid buzzwords: "synergy", "leverage", "ecosystem", "paradigm shift", "move the needle".
- Use plain language: "engineering team" not "talent ecosystem", "we can help" not "we can add value".

### 5. Respectful of the Prospect's Time
- Emails should be ≤ 150 words for cold outreach, ≤ 200 words for warm follow-up.
- One clear call to action per message.
- No multiple questions in a single message.

---

## Prohibited Phrases

The following phrases are banned from all outbound content:

- "I hope this finds you well"
- "Just following up"
- "Touching base"
- "Circle back"
- "Move the needle"
- "World-class"
- "Best-in-class"
- "Cutting-edge"
- "Synergy" / "synergies"
- "Leverage" (as a verb)
- "Ecosystem" (when referring to a company's team)
- "Aggressive hiring" (unless job_post_count ≥ 5 AND job_post_velocity_60d ≥ 3.0)

---

## Confidence-Gated Language Rules

| Signal Confidence | Required Phrasing Style | Example |
|---|---|---|
| high | Assertive | "You closed a $12M Series B in March." |
| medium | Assertive with attribution | "Crunchbase shows a $12M round closed in March." |
| low | Interrogative / conditional | "We noticed signals suggesting a recent funding event — is that right?" |
| null | Omit or ask | Do not reference the signal, or ask the prospect directly. |

---

## Scoring Threshold

A draft passes tone review if it scores ≥ 70/100 against these markers.
Drafts scoring below 70 are regenerated (maximum 2 retries before escalation to human review).
