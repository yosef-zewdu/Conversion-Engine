# Tenacious Tone Style Guide

This guide defines the required tone markers for all outbound content generated by the Conversion Engine.
Every draft is scored against these five markers before dispatch. Score < 4/5 on any marker → regenerate
(maximum 2 retries before escalation to human review). Numeric threshold: ≥ 70/100.

Authoritative source: `data/tenacious_sales_data/seed/style_guide.md`

---

## Core Tone Markers

### 1. Direct
- Clear, brief, actionable.
- Subject lines must start with one of: **Request:** / **Follow-up:** / **Context:** / **Question:**
- State the reason for contact in the first sentence. No preamble.

### 2. Grounded
- Every claim cites a specific signal from HiringSignalBrief.
- Use interrogative phrasing when signal confidence is low or null: "we noticed signals suggesting…"
- Exact figures preferred: "14 open engineering roles" not "many open roles".

### 3. Honest
- Refuse ungrounded claims.
- Say "we don't see public signal of X" rather than fabricating.
- Never over-interpret a weak signal.

### 4. Professional
- Avoid offshore-vendor jargon.
- Never use "bench" in prospect-facing copy — say "engineering team", "available capacity", or "engineers ready to deploy".
- Never use: "top talent", "world-class", "rockstar", "ninja".

### 5. Non-Condescending
- Frame competitor gaps as research findings, not prospect failures.
- Do not imply the prospect is behind or inadequate.
- Ask questions that give the prospect a chance to correct the framing.

## Format Constraints

- **Cold email body**: max 120 words
- **Warm follow-up**: max 200 words
- **One ask per message** — no multiple questions
- **Subject**: under 60 characters
- **No emojis** in cold outreach
- **No marketing taglines** in signatures

---

## Prohibited Phrases

- "I hope this finds you well"
- "Just following up"
- "Touching base" / "Circle back" / "Circling back"
- "Move the needle"
- "World-class" / "Best-in-class" / "Cutting-edge"
- "Synergy" / "synergies"
- "Leverage" (as a verb)
- "Ecosystem" (when referring to a company's team)
- "Bench" (in prospect-facing copy)
- "Aggressive hiring" (without data: requires open_roles_today ≥ 5 AND velocity_label in ["doubled", "tripled_or_more"])
- Subject lines starting with "Quick", "Just", "Hey", "Dear"

## Confidence-Gated Language Rules

| Signal Confidence | Required Phrasing Style | Example |
|---|---|---|
| high | Assertive | "You closed a $12M Series B in March." |
| medium | Assertive with attribution | "Crunchbase shows a $12M round closed in March." |
| low | Interrogative / conditional | "We noticed signals suggesting a recent funding event — is that right?" |
| null | Omit or ask | Do not reference the signal, or ask the prospect directly. |

### Hiring Velocity — velocity_label mapping

| velocity_label | Agent phrasing |
|---|---|
| tripled_or_more | Assertive: "Your engineering team has grown significantly in the last 60 days." |
| doubled | Assertive: "Your open engineering roles have roughly doubled in the last 60 days." |
| increased_modestly | Attribution: "Signals suggest modest engineering hiring growth." |
| flat | Omit unless used as a context framer. |
| declined | Omit; do not speculate on cause. |
| insufficient_signal | Ask rather than assert. Never invent a velocity claim. |

## Scoring Threshold

A draft passes tone review if it scores ≥ 70/100 against the five markers above.
Drafts scoring below 70 are regenerated (maximum 2 retries before escalation to human review).
